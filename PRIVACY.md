# Privacy policy: OpenClaw (personal assistant)

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

OpenClaw is a single-user, self-hosted assistant. The only user is the owner of this repository, who authorises it with their own Google account.

**What it accesses.** Only the Google data the owner explicitly grants on the consent screen. Depending on the scopes granted, that can include Gmail messages and labels, Google Calendar events (read-only), and Google Nest device status and settings.

**How the data is used.** Only to help the owner: summarising, searching, organising and drafting their email, reporting their calendar, and monitoring their home thermostat. The data is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

**Where it is stored.** OAuth tokens and credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's own computer. Processed data stays on that computer, except when the owner's assistant sends the owner a message on a channel the owner has configured.

**Third parties.** None, except the AI model provider the owner has configured to run the assistant, which processes request content to generate responses under that provider's terms.

**Revoking access.** The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and by deleting the stored tokens.

**Contact.** The repository owner, via GitHub.
